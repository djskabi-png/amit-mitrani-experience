About

A story that started with one small trick

Amit's curiosity began in childhood, after his father showed him a ring trick. Practice became performance, and performance became a language for connecting people through surprise, humour and participation. Today Amit is also one of the directors of the Israeli Magicians Association, connecting his stage work with the professional magic community.
